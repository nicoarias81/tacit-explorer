Wraps = public deposits (amount and asset are public by design). Cross-outs = a note cancelled against a deposit. These txs carry zero public amounts for leaves/nullifiers: the point of the design.

6 · The privacy frontier — what is public, what stays hidden

Tacit's privacy has a precise boundary. This page shows you the public side and refuses to fake the hidden side.

Public (on-chain, anyone can see)

  • That transactions exist, and when (timing, block heights, timestamps)
  • Counts: how many leaves, nullifiers, wraps, cross-outs, bridge burns/mints
  • Fees and tips paid to relayers and provers
  • Addresses that deposit (wrap) or receive withdrawals
  • Wrap amounts and assets (deposit sizes are public by design)
  • Memo bytes on leaves (encrypted payload — contents hidden, existence public)
  • The Merkle tree root and size

Hidden (only the note holder knows)

  • Note amounts inside leaves and spends — no public value for leaves or nullifiers
  • Who owns a leaf: commitments are opaque 32-byte values
  • The link between notes: a nullifier proves the right to spend, without saying which leaf
  • Where a withdrawn value came from or a deposit goes after shielding
  • The memos' decrypted meaning (only the view key can decrypt)
  • This is why a public explorer can show counts and totals but never a balance per person
